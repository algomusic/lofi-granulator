Updating LoFi Granulator Firmware:

1. Download the latest .bin from GitHub, https://github.com/algomusic/lofi-granulator
2. In a Chromium browser go to ESPConnect site, https://thelastoutpostworkshop.github.io/microcontroller_devkit/espconnect/
3. Connect the Granulator to an online computer via USB
4. In ESPConnect, click Connect and choose the JTAG/Serial connection from the popup dialog.
5. Click Flash Tools in eSportsConnect (left hand column), scroll to Flash Firmware
6. Drag the downloaded .bin file onto the Firmware Binary area of ESPConnect
7. In the Recommended Offset dropdown, choose App0 - 0x10000
8. Click the Flash Firmware button, and confirm Flash
9. The software will upload and the Granulator will restart.
